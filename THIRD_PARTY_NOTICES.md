## Stock media in templates

Templates link (they do not copy) photos and videos from these services, under their licences:

- **Unsplash** photos — https://unsplash.com/license
- **Picsum** placeholder photos (images from Unsplash) — https://picsum.photos
- **Mixkit** videos — https://mixkit.co/license/#videoFree

Site owners should replace stock media with their own before launch where a licence requires it.
